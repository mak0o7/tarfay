About Tarfay

An elegant coffee house with a gallery soul.

A room with texture

Every surface is imagined to feel calm and tactile: deep espresso tones, soft stone neutrals, brushed brass details, and lighting that flatters both coffee cups and framed work.

A home for local art

Tarfay pairs specialty coffee with rotating displays, artist pop-ins, and a setting that encourages guests to slow down and look a little longer.

What makes it memorable

  • Intimate seating for unhurried gatherings
  • Editorial styling with handcrafted details

Founder & CEO

A message from the young Saudi founder behind Tarfay.

Tarfay is imagined as more than a cafe. It is a warm cultural room in Al Khobar where coffee, softness, and art can live together.

"I wanted Tarfay to feel like a beautiful pause in the city. A place where specialty coffee, local creativity, and a gentle atmosphere could meet with sincerity."

Founder & CEO, Tarfay
